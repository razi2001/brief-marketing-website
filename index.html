    🐛 [P1] Payment Submission Failure in Checkout Flow: Potential Unhandled Promise Rejection in usePaymentIntent Leading to Silent Failure State

    📋 Summary

    Based on the reported behavior, the checkout payment flow appears to fail silently. This is most likely caused by an unhandled promise rejection in the payment confirmation pipeline, although it could also stem from a race condition between the PaymentElement mount lifecycle and the clientSecret fetch.

    🔍 Root Cause Analysis (hypothesized)

    The handleSubmit callback in src/features/checkout/components/CheckoutForm.tsx likely calls stripe.confirmPayment() without awaiting elements.submit(). The error may be swallowed by the onError handler in src/lib/api/payments.ts, leaving checkoutSlice.status stuck in 'pending'.

    🛠️ Proposed Fix
    // src/features/checkout/hooks/usePaymentIntent.ts
const { mutateAsync } = useMutation({
  mutationFn: confirmPaymentIntent,
  onError: (err: StripeError) => {
    dispatch(checkoutSlice.actions.setError(err.code));
    Sentry.captureException(err);
    throw err;
  },
});
    🧪 Testing Strategy
    • Mock @stripe/react-stripe-js with jest.mock and simulate card_declined
    • Add a Playwright E2E for the 3DS challenge path
    • Verify idempotency keys on retried POST /v1/payment_intents
    ⚠️ Risks

    Changing error propagation in the mutation layer may affect other consumers of usePaymentIntent, including the subscription upgrade flow, the BillingPortal retry banner and the legacy checkout-v1 fallback.

    ✔️ Acceptance Criteria
    • ☐ Payment errors surface to the user
    • ☐ No regression in 3DS flows
    • ☐ Sentry breadcrumbs include paymentIntent.id

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Do I need a coding agent or any setup?
    No. Brief Cloud writes and files the ticket for you. Create an account, connect your tracker, add the extension. That's it.
    What goes into a ticket?
    A clear title and summary, steps to reproduce, expected and actual, your video or screenshots, plus the console errors and failed network calls from the recording. Your ticket rules decide the labels and the style.
    Does it record all the time?
    No. Brief captures when you record or take a screenshot. Replay only keeps the last minute on sites your team adds, so you can catch a bug after it happens.
    What happens to my data?
    Your recording is used to write the ticket. Once it's filed, the audio and sampled frames are deleted. What you type is masked in the steps. No ads, no analytics in the extension, and we never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.
    Which browsers?
    Chrome on desktop, and other Chromium browsers like Edge, Brave and Arc.
